Honest answers

What happens after the 7 days?

Your card is charged for the plan you picked. Cancel before day 7 from your page and nothing is charged.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, any time from "Manage subscription" on your page. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades at the next renewal.

Do I need a shop connection?

It's the first step, and the one that makes every verdict reliable. Your shop's sales are what every ad gets judged on, and with no ads yet they are what Kalmly builds your first campaign from. If you only connect Meta, Kalmly judges on Meta's own numbers and says so on every verdict.

Why is there no "we do it for you" plan?

Because nobody at Kalmly should be deciding your budgets for you. Every plan tells you the exact edit and why. You make it yourself, or switch on approvals and tap Approve; either way you saw the reason first, and after a month you will know your account better than any agency does.

Is there a plan for agencies with more than 10 clients?

Email hello@kalmly.io; we'll set it up by hand.
