Honest answers

What happens after the 7 days?

Your card is charged for the plan you picked. Cancel before day 7 from your page and nothing is charged.

Can I switch plans?

Yes, any time from "Manage subscription" on your page. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades at the next renewal.

Do I need a shop connection?

No. Without one, Kalmly judges on Meta's own numbers and says so. With one, every verdict is based on real sales, which is what makes it reliable.

Why is there no "we do it for you" plan?

Because the moment we touch your budgets, you stop understanding your own ads. Every plan tells you the exact edit and why; making it takes you one minute in Ads Manager, and after a month you will know your account better than any agency does.

Email hello@kalmly.io; we'll set it up by hand.
