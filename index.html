Honest answers

Do I give you my Facebook login?

No. You connect with view rights only. No password, no edit rights, removable in one click.

Does it change my ads for me?

No. Kalmly tells you the exact edit and the reason; you make it in Ads Manager.

Why read-only, forever?

The moment software can edit a budget, a wrong guess costs real money and you cannot see why it happened. Kalmly makes the case with the numbers, in one sentence, and you make the change. If it is ever wrong, you will know before it costs you anything. That is a promise, not a roadmap item.

Why Meta only?

Because a shallow tool for five platforms helps nobody. Meta is where most small businesses spend, and judging those ads on real sales properly, with learning phases, frequency and per-city budgets, takes all of our attention. If Google or TikTok ever get the same depth, you will read it here first.

Why one decision instead of a dashboard of charts?

Ads Manager already is a dashboard of charts, and it was that dashboard that had me making a nervous edit every morning. What I needed at eight o'clock was one sentence: change this, or leave it. The numbers are still there if you want them; the sentence comes first.

— Maroussia, founder

Who is it for?

Businesses that run their own Meta ads at €10 to €200 a day and want more sales from them: tours, venues, shops, studios, e-commerce. Also for anyone paying an agency and wondering what they get for it.

What happens after the 7 days?

Your card is charged for the plan you picked. We email you on day 6; cancel before day 7 from your page and nothing is charged.

Do I need a shop connection?

No. Without one, Kalmly judges on Meta's own numbers and says so. With one, every verdict is based on real sales, which is what makes it reliable.
