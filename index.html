Honest answers.

Do I give you my Facebook login?

No. You connect with view rights only. No password, no edit rights, removable in one click.

Does it change my ads for me?

Only if you ask it to. By default Kalmly is view-only: it tells you the exact edit and the reason, and you make it in Ads Manager. Switch on approvals and share your ad account with "Manage campaigns" instead of "View performance". Each proposed pause, restart, budget change, new campaign or new ad set then gets an Approve button. New campaigns and ad sets are always created paused, and Kalmly never creates an ad, because that needs your creative. Raises are capped at 25 % and one change per ad set every three days. One tap and Kalmly makes the change. Without the tap, nothing happens. You can switch approvals off any time.

What is the first look?

The audit Kalmly runs the moment you connect: every campaign, ad set and ad, judged on your last seven days of real orders. It comes back as a plan, keep, change or new per ad set, with budgets and a one-line brief for each new creative, so you start with a structure instead of a blank dashboard.

Why does a human stay on the button?

The moment software edits a budget on its own, a wrong guess costs real money and you cannot see why it happened. So Kalmly always makes its case with the numbers first, in one sentence. You make the change, or you tap Approve; either way you saw the reason before anything moved.

Why Meta only?

Because a shallow tool for five platforms helps nobody. Meta is where most of the money goes, and judging those ads on real sales properly, with learning phases, frequency and per-segment budgets, takes all of our attention. If Google or TikTok ever get the same depth, you will read it here first.

Why one decision instead of a dashboard of charts?

Ads Manager already is a dashboard of charts, and it was that dashboard that had me making a nervous edit every morning. What I needed at eight o'clock was one sentence: change this, or leave it. The numbers are still there if you want them; the sentence comes first.

Maroussia, founder

Who is it for?

Anyone who runs Meta ads and wants every decision backed by real sales: the owner doing it alone, the in-house marketer, the ad expert who wants a second opinion every morning. It works for budgets from €10 a day to five figures a month. It is also useful if you pay an agency and want to check the work.

What happens after the 7 days?

Your card is charged for the plan you picked. We email you on day 6; cancel before day 7 from your page and nothing is charged.

Do I need a shop connection?

It's the first step, and the one that makes every verdict reliable. Your shop's sales are what every ad gets judged on, and with no ads yet they are what Kalmly builds your first campaign from. If you only connect Meta, Kalmly judges on Meta's own numbers and says so on every verdict.
