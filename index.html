Honest answers.

Does Kalmly change my ads without asking?

No. By default it only reads: it names the exact change and you make it in Ads Manager. If you switch on approvals, nothing changes without your tap: one change a day, never more than 20% on a budget.

What does it need access to?

View rights to your Meta ad account, and a connection to your shop or booking tool to read your sales. No password. Edit rights only if you switch on approvals. Remove access in one click.

What if I have no campaigns yet?

Connect your shop, booking tool or lead source. Kalmly plans one campaign per place, a broad ad set with a few ads, and one set for past visitors. Approve it and Kalmly creates it switched off; you switch it on. The photos and videos come from you.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time, from your page. The first 7 days are free and we email you on day 6. Cancel before day 7 and nothing is charged.
