The service

Kalmly gives you a first look at your Meta ad account, a short update every morning, answers to your questions, and concrete actions, all judged on the real results you connect (shop orders, bookings, leads or website visits) and on the goal you set. It is advice, generated automatically from your numbers. You decide what to do with it.

Your account

One account per business, used by the people who run that business. Your personal page link is your login; you are responsible for keeping it private. You must have the right to connect the ad account, shop or lead source you connect, and the right to accept these terms on behalf of your business.

Trial, price and payment

You enter a card when you sign up. The first 7 days are free. We email you on day 6. If you have not cancelled by the end of day 7, the plan you picked is charged on day 8 and renews every month, or every year if you chose yearly, until you cancel. Prices are shown on kalmly.io and at checkout and exclude VAT; VAT is added at checkout where it applies. Payment is handled by Stripe. If we change a price, we email you at least 30 days before it applies to you; you can cancel before then.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your page ("Manage subscription"), with no notice period and no email to anyone. Cancel during the trial and nothing is charged. Cancel later and the subscription ends at the end of the period you have paid for; there is no refund for the remaining days of a started period, except where the law gives you one. Upgrades apply immediately; downgrades at the next renewal.

Approve-to-apply

If you switch on approvals, Kalmly makes a change in your ad account only after you tap Approve on that specific change, on your instruction. Each suggestion is re-checked against your live numbers at the moment you tap, and the change is made exactly as shown: one change a day, never more than 20% on a budget, never on an ad set edited in the last 3 days or still learning, never pausing or editing a live ad, never switching on something that spends, and nothing while a balance or payment alert is open. A suggestion expires after 24 hours. You can undo any change in Ads Manager, switch approvals off, or remove Kalmly's permission in Meta Business Settings at any time. Kalmly is not responsible for the results of approved changes, for Meta changing its systems, or for changes you made yourself in between.

Advice, not a guarantee

We work to keep the service available every day and to base every check on the latest data we can read. We cannot promise results: advertising outcomes depend on your market, your creatives, Meta's systems and your decisions. The goal you set is a target, not a promise. Kalmly is not liable for business losses, lost profit or decisions you take. Our total liability to you is limited to the amount you paid us in the twelve months before the claim, except for damage caused intentionally or by gross negligence, where the law does not allow a limit.

Fair use

Each plan covers the ad accounts, shops and question volume stated on the pricing page. We may slow down or pause an account that uses the service in a way that harms it or other customers, or that breaks Meta's or your platform's terms. You may not resell the service or share one account between unrelated businesses.

Your data

Your data stays yours. How we handle it is described in the privacy policy; for the data about your own customers that passes through Kalmly, the data processing agreement applies. Both are part of these terms.

Ending the service

You can delete your account at any time from your page. We may end the service with 30 days' notice, refunding any prepaid period beyond that date, or immediately if these terms are seriously breached.
