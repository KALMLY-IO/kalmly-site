What Kalmly is

Kalmly reads a business's Meta (Facebook and Instagram) advertising data and, if the business connects it, its real results: shop orders, bookings, leads or website visits. From that it writes a first look at the account, a short morning update every day, answers to your questions in a chat, and a list of concrete actions. By default it only reads. If you switch on approvals, it makes one change in your ad account only after you tap Approve on that specific change.

Data about your customers

Order, booking and lead data may show what your customers bought or asked for. We ask your shop and your lead source only for the fields we need (date, amount, product or form name) and never for your customers' names, addresses or payment details. Where a lead source gives us more than that (a CRM record, for instance), we keep only the fields above. For this data you are the controller and Kalmly is your processor; the data processing agreement covers it. You are responsible for mentioning Kalmly in your own privacy notice if your law requires it.

Legal basis

Performing our contract with you (running your account and writing your checks) and our legitimate interest in keeping the service secure and improving it. Where we rely on your consent (linking Telegram, reading your Instagram posts, anonymous benchmarks), you can withdraw it at any time from your page.

Benchmarks. We may show you anonymous medians across many Kalmly customers (for example a typical click rate for shops of a similar size). No business can be identified from them; a median is only produced from a group of at least ten businesses.

Transfers outside the EU

Your data is stored in the EU. Anthropic (US) receives the numbers of each check while it writes the words, under the EU standard contractual clauses and, where it applies, the EU–US Data Privacy Framework. Stripe and Telegram apply their own published safeguards. Nothing else leaves the EU.

How long we keep it

As in the table above. When you delete your account, your settings, checks, chat, actions and keys are removed within 24 hours and backups are overwritten within 30 days. When you disconnect an ad account or a shop, its key is revoked immediately and the summaries already written stay until you delete the account.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export or delete your data at any time: most of it from your own Kalmly page ("Delete my account" is there), the rest by emailing hello@kalmly.io. You can disconnect your ad account or shop at any moment, from your page or from Meta's Business Settings. You can object to processing based on our legitimate interest, and you can withdraw consent where we rely on it. We answer within 30 days.

Security

Connection keys and settings are encrypted at rest; all traffic is encrypted in transit; access to the servers is limited to the people who run Kalmly, with two-factor login. One customer can never see another customer's accounts or numbers. Your personal page link is your login: keep it private, and ask for a new one from the login page if it has been shared. If a breach ever affects your data, we tell you within 72 hours of learning about it.

Cookies

kalmly.io sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising trackers. The app sets no cookies either: your page link is your login. Fonts are served from kalmly.io itself. That is why there is no cookie banner.

Contact and complaints

Questions or requests about your data: hello@kalmly.io. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Dutch data protection authority, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or to the authority in your own EU country.

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we email you first.
